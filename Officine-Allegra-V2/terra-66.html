01.Engineered Features

Built for
the ritual.

Six features, six gestures — every component engineered to serve the barista's craft without getting in the way.

TERRA
  1. 01

    Flat Burrs · 66 mm

    — the heart of the grinder.

    66 mm flat burrs, ground and matched in-house, running in a chamber built to hold them aligned. The particle distribution they produce is what reaches the cup.

  2. 02

    Micrometric Grind Adjustment

    — stepless, repeatable, precise.

    A stepless collar with a reference scale. Find the setting, note it, return to it after changing coffee — no detents to fight, no guessing between shots.

  3. 03

    Grind-by-Weight Dosing

    — dose by mass, not by time.

    Set the target weight once and reproduce it shot after shot. Dosing by mass removes the drift that timed grinding picks up as beans age.

  4. 04

    Portafilter Fork & Spout

    — grounds where they belong.

    An adjustable fork holds the basket while the grinder works, so both hands stay free. The spout keeps the dose in the basket and off the counter.

  5. 05

    Hopper & Single-Dose

    — bean management, either way.

    Run a full hopper with the shutter closed through service, or single-dose straight into the chamber when the coffee changes through the day.

  6. 06

    Motor & Acoustics

    — torque without the noise.

    Torque held at the finest settings, thermal behaviour that stays predictable across a full shift, and a noise level measured at the working position.
