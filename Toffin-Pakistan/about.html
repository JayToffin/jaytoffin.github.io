About Us

Nearly Two Decades Growing Indonesia's Café Industry

Our journey from one city to 19, from our first installation to thousands of machines running every day, reflects our commitment to building trust consistently — one partnership at a time.

Our Story

It All Began with a Commitment

Toffin Pakistan was founded in 2007 on one fundamental principle: every F&B business in Indonesia deserves access to international-quality coffee machines, backed by a reliable and professional service ecosystem.

Nearly two decades on, our role has grown well beyond conventional distribution. Toffin now stands as the most complete HORECA service ecosystem in Indonesia — connecting thousands of cafés and restaurants with the latest machine technology, premium-quality ingredients, an integrated digital platform and experienced specialists.

Being recognised as a global top-five distributor for several of the world's premium coffee machine brands was never our main goal — it is the natural result of consistently honouring the commitment we have held from the start: to be a partner that delivers real value to every business we serve.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Toffin Pakistan?

Toffin Pakistan is a distributor and strategic partner for F&B/HORECA businesses, established in 2007. Toffin provides integrated solutions, from premium coffee machines and ingredients to after-sales service, with a service and showroom network across 19 cities in Indonesia. Toffin is also recognised as a global top-5 distributor for several of the world's premium brands.

When was Toffin Pakistan established?

Toffin Pakistan was established in 2007 and has led the Indonesian HORECA industry for more than 17 years. Over that time, Toffin has grown from a coffee machine supplier into a long-term business partner for the F&B industry throughout Indonesia.

What products and solutions does Toffin provide?

Toffin provides a complete ecosystem for F&B businesses — not just coffee machines, but also premium espresso machines and grinders, more than 340 ingredient SKUs such as specialty coffee beans, syrups and beverage bases, after-sales service by certified technicians, barista training and business consultancy, plus Toffin App, a B2B digital platform for café operations.

Who are Toffin's customers, and do you serve hotels, restaurants and cafés?

Toffin's customers include more than 15,000 HORECA businesses in Indonesia, including cafés, restaurants and hotels, from independent businesses to multi-outlet chains. Toffin supports their entire cycle of needs, from machine selection and regular ingredient supply to training and technical support.

In which cities does Toffin have branches, and do you serve all of Indonesia?

Toffin has a service and showroom network in 19 cities in Indonesia: Jakarta, Bogor, Bandung, Cirebon, Semarang, Jogjakarta, Surabaya, Malang, Bali, Lombok, Medan, Pekanbaru, Batam, Palembang, Lampung, Pontianak, Banjarmasin, Samarinda and Makassar. Through this network, together with its technician teams and delivery services, Toffin can serve HORECA customers throughout Indonesia, both in branch cities and beyond.

Does Toffin have its own team of technicians, and how large is it?

Toffin has its own after-sales team of more than 100 certified technicians spread across 19 cities. The team handles installation, calibration, preventive maintenance and spare-part replacement.

Does Toffin provide training, education and coffee business consultancy?

Toffin offers training and consultancy programmes, from basic barista training and beverage menu development with the Flavour Engineer team to business consultancy on machine selection and café operations strategy. These services are designed to help F&B business owners improve the quality and consistency of their products.

Which international brands does Toffin distribute?

Toffin is the official distributor of dozens of international brands, including Victoria Arduino, Nuova Simonelli, Eversys, Rocket Espresso, SMEG, Eureka, Compak, VBM, Officine Allegra, Moccamaster Technivorm, Vitamix, Hario, Fabbri, Davinci Gourmet, FO and Comprital. Every product comes with an official manufacturer's warranty and genuine spare-parts support in Indonesia.

Can Toffin help me open a new coffee shop?

Yes. Toffin can support the opening of a new coffee shop from the very start — from concept consultation and choosing machines to match your capacity, to menu development and ingredient supply, barista training and after-sales technical support. Toffin's approach is tailored to the specific needs of each business.

What sets Toffin apart from other coffee suppliers?

Toffin's strength lies in its integrated solutions and extensive after-sales network. Toffin is a global top-5 distributor for several of the world's premium brands, backed by more than 100 certified technicians in 19 cities, availability of genuine spare parts, more than 340 ingredient SKUs, and Toffin App, winner of Product of the Year 2024.
