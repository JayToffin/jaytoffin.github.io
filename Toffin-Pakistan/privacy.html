Privacy Policy

Updated 21 May 2026

How Toffin Pakistan collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Toffin Pakistan ("Toffin", "we") respects your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal data you provide through the contact form on this website, in accordance with applicable data protection laws and regulations in Pakistan.

1. Data We Collect

When you complete the contact form, we collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Business name (café, restaurant, or hotel)
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • The content of your message / the requirements you submit

2. Purpose of Data Use

The data you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your questions, consultations, or requests for information
  • Follow up on business matters relating to the requirements you have submitted
  • Send relevant product or service offers (only with your consent)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on a restricted basis in our internal systems and can only be accessed by the team authorised to handle inquiries. We apply reasonable security standards to protect data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

4. Data Sharing

Toffin does not sell your personal data to third parties. Data may only be shared with principal partners or service providers where necessary to fulfil your request (e.g. coordinating machine installation or technical service), with your consent.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data from our systems
  • Withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time

6. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses standard analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. These cookies do not store personal data that can identify you.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or data rights requests may be sent to toffinpakistan@gmail.com or via WhatsApp to +92 309 1115951.
