Privacy & Data Protection

Toffin Privacy Policy

Toffin Pakistan ("Toffin", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains how we collect, use and protect the personal data you provide through the contact form on this website.

1. Data We Collect

When you complete the contact form, we collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Business name (café, restaurant or hotel)
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • The content of your message / the requirements you share with us

2. How We Use Your Data

The data you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your questions, consultation requests or requests for information
  • Follow up on business matters relating to the requirements you have shared
  • Send you relevant product or service offers (only with your consent)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on a restricted basis within our internal systems and can only be accessed by team members authorised to handle enquiries. We apply reasonable security standards to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

4. Data Sharing

Toffin does not sell your personal data to third parties. Data may only be shared with principal partners or service providers where necessary to fulfil your request (e.g. coordinating a machine installation or technical service), and only with your consent.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data
  • Request the deletion of your data from our systems
  • Withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time

6. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses standard analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. These cookies do not store personal data that can identify you.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or data rights requests can be sent to toffinpakistan@gmail.com or via WhatsApp to +92 309 1115951.
