About Toffin

More Than Just a Distributor

Since 2007, Toffin has been more than a coffee machine supplier — we are a long-term business partner to cafés, restaurants and hotels. From our office in Islamabad, Toffin Pakistan brings that same commitment to F&B businesses across Pakistan.

We understand that every café has its own story, vision and challenges. That is why our approach is never one-size-fits-all: from the initial consultation and machine recommendations to barista training and after-sales support — everything is tailored to the specific needs of your business.

As an official distributor of world-class coffee brands, we see every partnership as a commitment — trust built one café, restaurant and hotel at a time.
