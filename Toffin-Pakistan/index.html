About Toffin

More Than Just a Distributor

Since 2007, Toffin has been more than a coffee machine supplier — we are a long-term business partner to thousands of cafés, restaurants and hotels in Indonesia.

We understand that every café has its own story, vision and challenges. That is why our approach is never one-size-fits-all: from the initial consultation and machine recommendations to barista training and after-sales support — everything is tailored to the specific needs of your business.

Being recognised as a top-5 global distributor for several of the world's premium brands is more than a business achievement — it is proof of trust built one partnership at a time.
